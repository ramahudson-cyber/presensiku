## Problem
Gradient hero card header in `LeaveRequestPage.jsx` is not visible on mobile. The dark background, stat cards, and form card all render correctly, but the top gradient header ("Izin & Sakit" with purple gradient) is hidden.

## Root Cause
`LeaveRequestPage` renders its gradient header inside the AdminLayout's `<main>` which has `padding: 12px` on mobile. The header has `padding-top: 48px` but its outer wrapper has no positioning (`position: absolute` or similar). This means the gradient header starts ~12px from the top of the viewport, with 48px of internal padding pushing its content further down. The net visible gradient area is extremely small — practically invisible on mobile.

Compare with `EmployeeDashboard` which uses `absolute top-0 left-0` on its outer wrapper, positioning the header at the very top of the viewport.

## Fix
**In `LeaveRequestPage.jsx`** — match the pattern from `EmployeeDashboard.jsx`:

1. Add `absolute top-0 left-0` and `pb-24` (for bottom nav clearance) to the outermost wrapper `<div>`, matching EmployeeDashboard's approach
2. Add `min-h-screen w-full bg-transparent text-white font-sans` classes for consistent full-height rendering

This will position the header at the very top of the viewport, allowing the full 48px top padding gradient to be visible, with content flowing naturally below it.

## Files Changed
- `src/pages/employee/LeaveRequestPage.jsx` — one line change (outermost `<div>` styling)

## No other files need changes
- AdminLayout, BottomNav, Sidebar — no modifications needed
- The fix only adds positioning to the LeaveRequestPage's own wrapper
